A pre-soaked toner pad designed to sweep away dulling build-up and leave pores looking clearer and less noticeable. Each pad carries AHA and BHA for gentle exfoliation alongside panthenol, allantoin and sodium hyaluronate, so skin is left comfortable rather than stripped.

Key Benefits

  • Sweeps away dulling surface build-up.
  • Helps pores look clearer and less noticeable.
  • Exfoliates gently with AHA and BHA.
  • Cushions the exfoliation with panthenol and allantoin.
  • Leaves skin feeling hydrated with sodium hyaluronate.
  • 70 pads per jar, ready to use with no decanting.

How to Use

After cleansing, take one pad and sweep it gently over the face, avoiding the eye and lip areas. Follow with the rest of your routine. Start two or three times a week and build up as your skin allows, and wear sunscreen during the day.

Suitable For

Combination and oily skin, especially those concerned with visible pores, uneven texture or dullness.
