Where we deliver

We deliver across South Africa. Tell us your suburb and city when you order and we will confirm whether a courier reaches you and what it costs.

What delivery costs

Delivery is quoted per order, because the cost depends on your address and the size of the parcel. You will always be told the delivery cost on WhatsApp before you pay anything. Nothing is added after you have paid.

How long it takes

Orders are dispatched once payment reflects in our account, which is usually the same or the next business day for an EFT. From dispatch, main-centre deliveries typically arrive within 2 to 4 business days and outlying areas within 3 to 7. These are the courier's estimates rather than guarantees.

Tracking your order

Every order gets a reference in the form CAS-000000. Keep it: you can look it up on our Track Your Order page, and quoting it on WhatsApp is the fastest way for us to find you. Where the courier provides a tracking number, we pass it on.

If something goes wrong

If your parcel has not arrived in the expected window, message us on WhatsApp at {{WA_DISPLAY}} with your reference and we will chase the courier. If a parcel is lost in transit we will replace the order or refund you in full.

Checking your parcel

Please open your parcel and check the contents on the day it arrives. If anything is missing, damaged or not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days so we can put it right — see our Returns Policy.
