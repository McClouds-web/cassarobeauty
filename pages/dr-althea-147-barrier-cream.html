A rich barrier-supporting moisturiser designed to hydrate and comfort dry, moisture-depleted skin. Its formula combines ceramide, betaine and multiple forms of hyaluronic acid to help maintain moisture and support a healthy-looking skin barrier.

Key Benefits

  • Helps strengthen and support the skin’s moisture barrier.
  • Provides lasting hydration.
  • Helps reduce feelings of dryness and tightness.
  • Supports softer, smoother-feeling skin.
  • Contains multiple forms of hyaluronic acid.
  • Helps maintain a comfortable, well-moisturised complexion.

How to Use

Apply an appropriate amount as the final step of your skincare routine. Gently spread over the face and pat until absorbed. Use morning and evening as needed.

Suitable For

Normal to dry skin, particularly dehydrated or moisture-depleted skin in need of additional barrier support.
