The richest step in the Centella line. A dense, cushioned cream in an amber jar that pairs pure Madagascar Centella with probiotic ferments, made for skin that has gone dry, tight or reactive and needs putting back together.

Key Benefits

  • A rich, occlusive finish for skin that feels stripped or tight.
  • Pairs Centella asiatica with probiotic ferments.
  • Cushions the skin overnight, so mornings start calmer.
  • Comfortable on skin that reacts to most rich creams.
  • Amber glass jar keeps the formula out of the light.

How to Use

As the last step of the evening routine, take a small amount on a clean spatula or fingertip, warm it between the fingers and press it over the face and neck. On very dry skin it can be used morning and evening; under sunscreen, use less.

Suitable For

Normal, dry, combination and sensitive skin, especially anyone whose barrier feels compromised after actives, sun or cold weather.
