A creamy whip cleanser designed to remove daily impurities and excess sebum while leaving the skin feeling fresh and comfortably cleansed. Its formula features PDRN and niacinamide as key skincare ingredients for a refined, radiant-looking complexion.

Key Benefits

  • Gently cleanses away dirt, oil and daily impurities.
  • Creates a rich, whipped foam for thorough cleansing.
  • Contains niacinamide to support brighter, more even-looking skin.
  • Features PDRN as a key formula ingredient.
  • Leaves skin feeling clean and refreshed.
  • Suitable for daily cleansing.

How to Use

Dispense a small amount onto wet hands and work into a rich foam. Gently massage over damp face, avoiding the eye area, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and oily skin, particularly those concerned with dullness, uneven-looking tone or excess sebum.
