A refreshing foaming cleanser designed to remove excess oil, daily impurities and buildup while helping keep blemish-prone skin feeling balanced. Its formula combines heartleaf, Centella Asiatica and salicylic acid with gentle cleansing agents.

Key Benefits

  • Helps remove excess sebum and impurities.
  • Supports clearer-looking pores.
  • Contains salicylic acid for pore-focused cleansing.
  • Features Heartleaf Extract.
  • Includes Centella Asiatica and Panthenol.
  • Leaves skin feeling fresh and thoroughly cleansed.

How to Use

Dispense a small amount onto wet hands and work into a foam. Gently massage over damp skin, avoiding the eye area, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

Suitable For

Combination, oily and blemish-prone skin, especially those concerned with excess sebum or congested pores.
