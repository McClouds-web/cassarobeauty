A lightweight daily toner featuring Centella Asiatica from Madagascar to gently refresh and care for the skin. Designed to help smooth the complexion while leaving skin feeling calm, hydrated and ready for the next steps in your routine.

Key Benefits

  • Gently refreshes and tones the skin.
  • Helps remove residual buildup after cleansing.
  • Provides gentle exfoliating care.
  • Helps improve the appearance of uneven texture.
  • Centella Asiatica helps soothe and condition the skin.
  • Leaves skin feeling smooth and refreshed.

How to Use

After cleansing, apply an appropriate amount to a cotton pad or directly into your palms. Gently sweep or pat over the face and neck until absorbed. Follow with your serum and moisturiser.

Suitable For

Normal, combination, oily and sensitive skin. Suitable for those concerned with uneven texture, dullness or skin that benefits from gentle exfoliating care.
