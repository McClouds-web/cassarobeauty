A thin, closely-fitting sheet mask soaked in a light water essence. It clings to the face rather than sliding off, so twenty minutes is enough to leave skin feeling hydrated and calm. Choose from three variants.

Key Benefits

  • Sits close to the face instead of slipping.
  • Carries a light water-type essence rather than a heavy serum.
  • Leaves skin feeling hydrated and comfortable.
  • Skin irritation tested, as stated on the sachet.
  • An easy, inexpensive step to keep in the drawer.

How to Use

After cleansing and toning, unfold the sheet and smooth it over the face, lining up the eye and mouth openings. Leave on for fifteen to twenty minutes, remove and discard the sheet, then pat the remaining essence into the skin. Do not rinse.
