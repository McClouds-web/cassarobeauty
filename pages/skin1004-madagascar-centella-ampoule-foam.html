A gentle foaming cleanser designed to lift away impurities and excess oil while leaving skin feeling fresh and comfortable. Formulated with Centella Asiatica from Madagascar, it provides an effective cleansing step without leaving the skin feeling overly stripped.

Key Benefits

  • Cleanses away dirt, impurities and excess sebum.
  • Creates a rich, creamy foam for thorough cleansing.
  • Helps leave skin feeling fresh and clean.
  • Contains Centella Asiatica, known for its soothing properties.
  • Suitable for use as part of a daily skincare routine.

How to Use

Dispense a small amount onto wet hands and work into a rich lather. Gently massage over damp face, avoiding the eye area, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and oily skin, as well as those looking for a gentle everyday foaming cleanser.
