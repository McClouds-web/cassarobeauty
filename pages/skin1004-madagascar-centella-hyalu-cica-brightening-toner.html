A watery toner made with pure Centella from Madagascar, run through with hyaluronic acid. It sinks in the moment it touches the skin, leaves no film behind, and preps the face so everything after it spreads further and settles faster.

Key Benefits

  • Made with pure Centella asiatica from Madagascar.
  • Pairs Centella with hyaluronic acid on the first step of the routine.
  • Absorbs immediately and leaves no tacky film.
  • Softens the look of dullness and uneven tone over time.
  • Comfortable enough for skin that reacts easily.

How to Use

After cleansing, pour a little into the palms or onto a cotton pad and press it over the face and neck. Let it settle for a moment, then follow with an ampoule, serum or cream. Use morning and evening. It can also be layered two or three times on days when skin feels dry.

Suitable For

Normal, combination, dry and sensitive skin, especially anyone working on dullness or an uneven tone.
