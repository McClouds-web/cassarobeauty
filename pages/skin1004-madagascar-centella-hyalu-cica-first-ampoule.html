A first ampoule, meant to go on before everything else. It is thin enough to disappear into damp skin in seconds, carrying Centella and hyaluronic acid down to where the rest of the routine can build on them.

Key Benefits

  • Designed as the first treatment step, before serums and creams.
  • Thin, watery texture that absorbs almost immediately.
  • Made with pure Centella asiatica from Madagascar.
  • Hyaluronic acid to hold hydration in place through the day.
  • A generous 100 ml bottle for daily, twice-a-day use.

How to Use

After toner, while skin is still slightly damp, dispense two or three pumps and press it over the face and neck. Give it a moment to settle, then follow with an ampoule, serum or moisturiser. Use morning and evening.

Suitable For

Normal, combination, dry and sensitive skin, and anyone whose skin feels dehydrated rather than oily or dry.
