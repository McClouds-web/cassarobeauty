A lightweight daily sunscreen offering high-level SPF50+ PA++++ protection while keeping the skin comfortable and hydrated. The rice and probiotic-derived formula leaves a smooth, natural-looking finish suitable for everyday wear.

Key Benefits

  • Provides broad-spectrum SPF50+ PA++++ sun protection.
  • Helps protect skin from UVA and UVB exposure.
  • Lightweight, comfortable texture for daily use.
  • Helps maintain skin hydration.
  • Leaves skin feeling soft and nourished.

How to Use

Apply generously and evenly as the final step of your morning skincare routine, approximately 15 minutes before sun exposure. Reapply regularly, particularly after sweating, swimming or prolonged sun exposure.
