The short version

Cassaro Beauty does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies or analytics that follow you around the internet. We do not run a cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

What we do store

The site uses your browser's local storage — not cookies — to remember four things, all of them on your own device:

  • the items in your basket
  • the items on your wishlist
  • the delivery details you typed at checkout, so you do not retype them
  • the references of orders you have placed from this browser

None of this is sent to us until you place an order, and none of it identifies you to anyone else. Clearing your browsing data removes all of it, and the site keeps working — you simply start with an empty basket.

Third parties

We load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them. Following a link to our Instagram or TikTok takes you to those platforms, where their own policies apply.
