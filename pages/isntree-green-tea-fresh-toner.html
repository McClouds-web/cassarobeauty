A lightweight daily toner formulated with green tea extract to help refresh the skin, control excess sebum and support balanced hydration. It leaves the complexion feeling clean, comfortable and refreshed without a heavy finish.

Key Benefits

  • Helps control excess sebum.
  • Refreshes and lightly hydrates the skin.
  • Supports a more balanced skin feel.
  • Contains green tea extract from Jeju.
  • Helps leave skin feeling calm and comfortable.
  • Lightweight texture suitable for daily use.

How to Use

After cleansing, apply an appropriate amount to the face using your hands or a cotton pad. Gently pat into the skin until absorbed, then continue with serum and moisturiser.

Suitable For

Combination and oily skin, especially those concerned with excess sebum or a heavy skin feel.
