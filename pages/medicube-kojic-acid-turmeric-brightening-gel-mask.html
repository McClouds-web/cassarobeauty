A brightening gel mask formulated with kojic acid, turmeric and 2% niacinamide to help improve the appearance of dullness and uneven-looking skin tone. The moisture-rich gel format leaves skin feeling hydrated, refreshed and visibly radiant.

Key Benefits

  • Supports a more even-looking skin tone.
  • Provides a boost of moisture.
  • Contains 2% niacinamide.

After cleansing and toning, apply the gel mask evenly to the face. Leave on for the recommended time stated on the packaging, then remove and gently pat any remaining essence into the skin.
