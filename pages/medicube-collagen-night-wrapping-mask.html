An overnight wrapping mask designed to lock in moisture while helping skin feel smoother and firmer by morning. Formulated with collagen extract, niacinamide and Ceramide NP for hydrated, radiant-looking skin.

Key Benefits

  • Helps replenish and lock in moisture overnight.
  • Supports smoother, firmer-looking skin.
  • Helps improve the appearance of radiance.
  • Supports the skin’s moisture barrier.
  • Provides intensive overnight care.
  • Leaves skin feeling soft and hydrated.

How to Use

As the final step of your evening skincare routine, apply an even layer over the face, avoiding the eyes and lips. Allow it to dry before sleeping. In the morning, gently peel off the wrapping layer or remove according to the product directions.

Suitable For

Normal, dry and combination skin, particularly skin experiencing dryness, dullness or a lack of firmness.
