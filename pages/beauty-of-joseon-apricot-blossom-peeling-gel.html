A gel-type exfoliator with a soft, moist rolling texture. Formulated with cellulose and apricot flower water, it gently lifts away dead skin cells and the day's build-up, leaving skin refreshed and softer than before.

Key Benefits

  • Gently removes dead skin cells and surface build-up.
  • Rolls away on contact, so there is no harsh scrubbing.
  • Leaves skin feeling softer than before.
  • Formulated with apricot flower water.
  • Cellulose does the lifting in place of hard grains.

How to Use

Apply to dry, cleansed skin and massage gently in small circles, avoiding the eye and lip areas, until the gel rolls away. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water and follow with the rest of your routine. Use once or twice a week.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and oily skin, especially those concerned with rough texture, flakiness or a dull-looking complexion.
