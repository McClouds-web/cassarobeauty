A wash-off pore mask built around red bean, a grain used in Korean skincare for generations to refresh and clarify the skin. Smoothed over the face and rinsed away, it leaves the complexion feeling clean and looking calmer, with pores appearing less noticeable.

Key Benefits

  • Helps pores look clearer and less noticeable.
  • Leaves skin feeling refreshed rather than tight.
  • Sweeps away the day's build-up in one rinse.
  • Built around red bean, the ingredient the mask is named for.
  • A weekly step that suits an evening routine.

How to Use

Smooth an even layer over clean, dry skin, avoiding the eye and lip areas. Leave on for the time stated on the packaging, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water and follow with the rest of your routine.

Suitable For

Combination and oily skin, especially those concerned with visible pores, congestion or a dull-looking complexion.
