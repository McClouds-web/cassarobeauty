A creamy foaming cleanser designed to deeply cleanse the skin by removing impurities and excess sebum. Formulated with Centella Asiatica from Madagascar, it leaves skin feeling thoroughly cleansed and refreshed.

Key Benefits

  • Deeply cleanses dirt and daily impurities.
  • Helps remove excess sebum.
  • Helps keep pores feeling clean and refreshed.
  • Produces a rich, creamy cleansing foam.
  • Contains Centella Asiatica for its soothing properties.
  • Leaves skin feeling fresh after cleansing.

How to Use

Apply a small amount to wet hands and work into a rich lather. Gently massage over damp skin, avoiding the eye area, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

Suitable For

Combination and oily skin, particularly those concerned with excess sebum and congested pores.
