The clarifying toner in the Centella line. Tea-Trica pairs pure Madagascar Centella with tea tree, so it works on congestion and the redness that usually comes with it on the same step, without the sting of a stripping astringent.

Key Benefits

  • Made with pure Centella asiatica from Madagascar.
  • Tea tree for skin that runs congested or blemish-prone.
  • Clarifies without the tight, stripped feeling of an astringent.
  • Calms the look of redness around active breakouts.
  • A large 210 ml bottle, sized for twice-daily use.

How to Use

After cleansing, soak a cotton pad or use the palms and sweep the toner over the face, paying attention to the nose, chin and anywhere that congests. Follow with an ampoule or a light moisturiser. Use morning and evening.

Suitable For

Combination, oily and sensitive skin, particularly anyone dealing with congestion, breakouts or the redness left behind by them.
