A lightweight brightening serum formulated with rice-derived ingredients and alpha-arbutin to help improve the appearance of dullness and uneven-looking tone. It absorbs easily and layers well within a daily skincare routine.

Key Benefits

  • Helps improve the appearance of uneven skin tone.
  • Supports a brighter-looking complexion.
  • Helps reduce the look of dullness.
  • Features rice-derived ingredients.
  • Contains alpha-arbutin.
  • Lightweight texture suitable for daily layering.

How to Use

After cleansing and toning, apply 2–3 drops evenly across the face. Gently pat until absorbed, then follow with moisturiser. Use sunscreen during the day.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and dry skin, especially those concerned with dullness, uneven-looking tone or post-blemish marks.
