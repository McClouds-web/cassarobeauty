A double layer serum you spray rather than pour. The oil and water phases sit separated in the bottle until you shake them together, and the mist that comes out is enriched with white truffle from Italy. d'Alba puts the promise plainly on the label: hydrated and radiant skin.

Key Benefits

  • A double layer serum, oil and water, mixed at the moment of use.
  • Enriched with white truffle from Italy.
  • Sprays on, so it can be used over make-up during the day.
  • Leaves a soft radiance rather than a heavy oil finish.
  • Doubles as a light treatment step or a refresher.

How to Use

Shake the bottle until the two layers turn cloudy and combine. Hold about twenty centimetres from the face and spray two or three times, then press it in with the palms. Use after toner in the morning and evening, or over make-up through the day to refresh.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and dry skin, and anyone who wants a hydrating step that does not add another thick layer.
