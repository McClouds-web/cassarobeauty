Daily protection on a centella base. SPF50+ PA++++, the highest rating printed on Korean sunscreens, in a cream that goes on soft and finishes without the tackiness that usually stops people reapplying.

Key Benefits

  • SPF50+ PA++++, as printed on the tube.
  • Built on the same centella base as the rest of the mixsoon line.
  • Soft, comfortable finish rather than a heavy occlusive one.
  • Suits skin that reacts to stronger sunscreen formulas.
  • A 50 g tube for daily use.

How to Use

As the last step of the morning routine, apply generously to the face and neck about fifteen minutes before going outside. Reapply every two hours in strong sun, and after swimming or sweating.

Suitable For

Normal, combination, dry and sensitive skin, and anyone who wants a high-protection sunscreen that is calm enough for everyday use.
