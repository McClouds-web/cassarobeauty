A refreshing foam cleanser designed to thoroughly remove daily impurities while helping maintain comfortable hydration. Formulated with Sedum Sarmentosum Extract and multiple forms of hyaluronic acid, it leaves the skin feeling clean, soft and refreshed after cleansing.

Key Benefits

  • Thoroughly cleanses away daily impurities.
  • Helps remove excess oil and buildup.
  • Contains multiple forms of hyaluronic acid to support hydration.
  • Features Sedum Sarmentosum Extract.
  • Panthenol and allantoin help support a comfortable skin feel.
  • Leaves skin feeling fresh without an overly dry finish.

How to Use

Dispense a small amount onto wet hands and work into a rich foam. Gently massage over damp face, avoiding the eye area, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and oily skin, including those looking for a deep cleanser with hydration-supporting ingredients.
