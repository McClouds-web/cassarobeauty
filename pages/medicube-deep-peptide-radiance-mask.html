A moisture-rich sheet mask designed to hydrate and support a smooth, radiant-looking complexion. Infused with peptide-focused ingredients, niacinamide and panthenol, its creamy essence helps leave skin feeling soft, replenished and refreshed.

Key Benefits

  • Provides intensive moisture.
  • Helps support a radiant-looking complexion.
  • Helps improve the appearance of dullness.
  • Supports soft, smooth-feeling skin.
  • Contains niacinamide and panthenol.
  • Features a peptide complex.

How to Use

After cleansing and toning, unfold the mask and apply evenly over the face. Leave on for the recommended time stated on the packaging, then remove and gently pat the remaining essence into the skin.

Suitable For

Normal, dry, combination and dehydrated skin, especially those concerned with dullness or lack of moisture.
