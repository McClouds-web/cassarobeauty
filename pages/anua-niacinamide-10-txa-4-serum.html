A targeted brightening serum formulated with 10% niacinamide and 4% tranexamic acid to help improve the appearance of uneven tone and dullness. Its lightweight texture is designed for daily use as part of a balanced skincare routine.

Key Benefits

  • Helps improve the appearance of uneven skin tone.
  • Supports a brighter-looking complexion.
  • Helps reduce the look of dullness.
  • Contains 10% niacinamide.
  • Contains 4% tranexamic acid.
  • Lightweight serum texture suitable for layering.

How to Use

After cleansing and toning, apply a few drops evenly across the face. Gently pat until absorbed, then follow with moisturiser. Use sunscreen during the day.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and oily skin, especially those concerned with uneven-looking tone, dullness or post-blemish marks.
