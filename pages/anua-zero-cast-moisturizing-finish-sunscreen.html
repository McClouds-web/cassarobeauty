Broad spectrum SPF 50, water resistant for eighty minutes, and named for the thing most sunscreens get wrong on deeper skin: it goes on without leaving a cast. The finish is moisturising rather than matte, so it sits under make-up instead of fighting it.

Key Benefits

  • Broad Spectrum SPF 50, as printed on the tube.
  • Water resistant for 80 minutes.
  • No white cast, which is the point of the Zero-Cast name.
  • A moisturising finish rather than a dry, powdery one.
  • Sits under make-up without pilling or greying it.

How to Use

As the last step of the morning routine, apply generously to the face and neck about fifteen minutes before going outside. Reapply every two hours in strong sun, and after swimming, sweating or towel drying.

Suitable For

Normal, combination, dry and oily skin. Worth a look for anyone on a deeper skin tone who has given up on sunscreens that grey the face.
