A lightweight brightening serum formulated to help improve the appearance of dark spots and uneven-looking skin tone while supporting daily hydration. Its niacinamide-based formula is designed to leave the complexion looking clearer, smoother and more radiant.

Key Benefits

  • Helps improve the appearance of dark spots.
  • Supports a brighter, more even-looking complexion.
  • Contains 5% niacinamide.
  • Helps maintain skin moisture.
  • Lightweight texture suitable for daily use.
  • Supports a healthy-looking glow.

How to Use

After cleansing and toning, apply a small amount evenly over the face or targeted areas. Gently pat until absorbed, then follow with moisturiser. Use sunscreen during the day.

Suitable For

Normal, combination and oily skin, especially those concerned with dullness, uneven-looking tone or post-blemish marks.
